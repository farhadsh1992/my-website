# About Farhad Shadmand

## Bio / summary

Farhad Shadmand is an AI researcher and developer based in Coimbra, Portugal. He is a
post-doctoral researcher at VisTeam, Institute of Systems and Robotics – Coimbra (ISR), where he
works on generative video models, including video watermarking and diffusion-based generation
(Stable Diffusion). He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering and Intelligent Systems from the
University of Coimbra, and has hands-on experience building deep learning models based on
diffusion models, GANs, and VLM-based systems on multi-GPU platforms (CUDA). His work has led
to multiple publications, including CVPR, WACV, and IEEE Access, and one granted patent
(with the European Patent Office and in Portugal) on steganography-based document security. He
brings strong skills in PyTorch, computer vision, model optimization, and AI agents.

He is originally from Isfahan, Iran, and is a Portuguese citizen. His native language is
Persian; he is fluent in English and a beginner in Portuguese.
